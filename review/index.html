01What to review

Your changes, a branch, a commit, or everything.

Every review command takes the same targets. Run them all with /review, or just the one you need.

/reviewYour uncommitted changes
/review mainEverything on this branch that isn't on main
/review a1b2c3dOne commit
/review --allThe whole project, not just a change
/review-securityOnly the security reviewer. /review-quality, /review-practices and /review-rules work the same way
02The reviewers
The brief

Reads the project once and writes what every reviewer shares: where untrusted input comes in, the security patterns in use, and the conventions.

Security

Looks for vulnerabilities the change introduces, one class at a time, and sorts the hits from the dependency and secret scanners.

Quality

Finds correctness and maintainability problems the change introduces.

Practices

Finds departures from the best practices of your language and framework.

Rules

Checks the change against .koda/review-rules.md, AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md, and nothing else. Add a review-rules.md of your own on top.

The challenger

Tries to prove each finding wrong. Anything that scores under your threshold is dropped before you see it.
