Goals

Give your agents a goal. Not a babysitter.

Agree on the plan once. Then walk away.

Shape the plan with your agent: the steps, the files it may change, and how it will prove the work is done. Hand it off, and KodaCode carries it through on its own while you focus on what’s next.

Your work stays untouched.

Every goal runs in its own copy of your project, so your branch and your uncommitted changes stay exactly as you left them.

Nothing lands without you.

The agent checks its work against the criteria you agreed. When it’s done, review the result, then merge it as a single commit or discard it.

Come back to finished work, not a list of questions.

01The plan

Nothing is written until you agree.

The plan lists each step, the files it will touch and how the result will be checked. Tell it what to change as often as you like. Each new version marks the steps that are new or different, so you don't have to reread the whole thing. Accepted plans are saved in your project, under .koda/plans.

02Its own copy

It works in a copy, so your files stay put.

An accepted plan runs in a separate copy of your project, on its own branch. Your checkout, your editor and anything you haven't committed are left alone. A bar above the session shows how it's going and how much it has spent. Pause it, pick it back up, or just leave it running while you're away.

03Landing it

One commit, or nothing at all.

When it's done, it waits for you. Merge adds all of its changes as a single commit, and Discard throws the copy away. Tick "Merge when done" on the plan and it merges by itself as soon as it finishes. If something's in the way, like a conflict or a commit you made in the meantime, your project is left exactly as it was and the goal tells you what happened.
